Welcome to GIS

Gosain International School (GIS) is a distinguished educational institution with a legacy of academic excellence spanning over 18 years. As a group of CBSE affiliated schools across Odisha, we are committed to nurturing young minds with a perfect blend of traditional values and modern education.

Our philosophy centers on holistic development, fostering intellectual curiosity, creativity, and social responsibility in every student. With four state-of-the-art campuses across Odisha, we provide world-class infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a curriculum that prepares students for global challenges.
