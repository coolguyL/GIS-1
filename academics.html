Comprehensive Curriculum

CBSE affiliated curriculum with focus on holistic development

Play Group

Early stimulation through play-based learning and sensory activities for toddlers.

  • Sensory Play
  • Fine Motor Skills
  • Language Development
  • Social Interaction
  • Rhythm & Music

Nursery

Developing basic cognitive skills, communication abilities, and social awareness.

  • Shapes & Colors
  • Pre-Writing Skills
  • Story Time
  • Puzzle Solving
  • Creative Arts

LKG (Lower Kindergarten)

Introduction to formal learning concepts through interactive and fun activities.

  • Alphabet Recognition
  • Number Concepts (1-50)
  • Basic English & Odia
  • Nature Awareness
  • Physical Activities

UKG (Upper Kindergarten)

Advanced preparation for formal schooling with comprehensive skill development.

  • Reading Readiness
  • Basic Mathematics
  • Writing Skills
  • General Knowledge
  • Creative Expression

Primary School (I-V)

Foundational learning with emphasis on literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness.

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies
  • Art & Craft
  • Music & Dance

Middle School (VI-VIII)

Subject-based learning with introduction to sciences, social sciences, and languages.

  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Social Science
  • Computer Science
  • Third Language (Sanskrit/Hindi)
  • Physical Education

Examination System

Comprehensive continuous assessment pattern for academic growth from Pre-Primary to Class 8

Academic Assessment Schedule 2025-26

Pre-Primary (Play Group - UKG)

Continuous Assessment Pattern
  • Daily observation & activity-based evaluation
  • Monthly progress reports shared with parents
  • Quarterly developmental assessments
  • No formal written examinations
Key Assessment Periods
Quarterly Review 1: August 15-20, 2025
Quarterly Review 2: November 10-15, 2025
Quarterly Review 3: February 5-10, 2026
Final Assessment: March 15-20, 2026

Primary & Middle (Class I-VIII)

Term-based Examination Pattern
  • Two major terms with comprehensive examinations
  • Regular periodic tests throughout the year
  • Project-based assessments for practical subjects
  • Co-curricular activities evaluation

Detailed Examination Schedule 2025-26 (Class I-VIII)

Class Periodic Test 1 Term 1 Exams Periodic Test 2 Term 2 Exams
Class I-II Aug 5-7, 2025 Sep 25-30, 2025 Dec 10-12, 2025 Feb 20-25, 2026
Class III-V Aug 10-12, 2025 Oct 1-6, 2025 Dec 15-17, 2025 Feb 25-Mar 2, 2026
Class VI-VII Aug 15-17, 2025 Oct 5-10, 2025 Dec 18-20, 2025 Mar 3-8, 2026
Class VIII Aug 18-20, 2025 Oct 8-13, 2025 Dec 20-22, 2025 Mar 5-10, 2026

Assessment Pattern & Weightage (Class I-VIII)

Formative Assessment (40%)
  • Periodic Tests (20%): Two written tests per term
  • Multiple Assessments (15%):
    • Subject Enrichment Activities
    • Portfolio Assessment
    • Practical Work
    • Project Work
  • Notebook Submission (5%): Regularity, neatness, completion
Summative Assessment (60%)
  • Term 1 Examination (30%): Comprehensive written exam
  • Term 2 Examination (30%): Final written exam
  • Special Features:
    • Objective & subjective questions
    • Practical assessments for Science & Computer
    • Speaking & listening tests for languages
Important Notes
Minimum 75% attendance required for all exams
Progress reports issued after each term
Parent-teacher meetings after each assessment
Remedial classes for students needing support
